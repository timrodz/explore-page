# Kiki Explore

Vocabulary for the property booking explore MVP: guests search London properties and view details; booking is a placeholder only.

## Language

**Host**:
A person who owns one or more properties offered on the platform.
_Avoid_: Owner, landlord, supplier

**Property**:
A bookable place in London with a title, description, nightly price, and neighbourhood area.
_Avoid_: Listing, stay, rental, accommodation

**Property listing**:
A date window during which a property is available to book; a guest stay must fall entirely inside one window.
_Avoid_: Availability block, calendar entry, reservation

**Area**:
A London neighbourhood label used for location filtering; one of Shoreditch, Camden, Greenwich, Clapham, Notting Hill, or Canary Wharf.
_Avoid_: City, location (when meaning only “London”), postcode

**Stay**:
The guest-selected check-in and check-out dates used to filter properties (check-out day is exclusive).
_Avoid_: Booking, trip, reservation
